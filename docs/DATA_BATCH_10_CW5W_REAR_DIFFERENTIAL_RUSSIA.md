# Data batch 10 — CW5W / 4B12 Russia: rear differential lubrication evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**Scope:** Mitsubishi Outlander XL CW5W / 4B12 / 4WD; Russian-market applicability still requires exact model-year and unit confirmation. Evidence ledger only; not approved for production app data.

## Separate service point

The rear differential is a separate assembly from the W5MBB manual gearbox and the transfer case. Do not share fluid labels, quantities, or service instructions between these assemblies.

## Candidate values found

A third-party-hosted copy of the Outlander XL PDI / periodic maintenance manual contains a distinct rear-differential oil replacement procedure. It lists:

- Oil specification: API GL-5, SAE 80W.
- Quantity: 0.5 L.
- Filling instruction: fill to the lower edge of the filler-plug hole.

Source: [Outlander XL PDI / periodic maintenance manual PDF copy](https://mmc-manuals.ru/manuals/outlander_xl/online/Service_Manual/spec/pdf/4753343558CW8W_08_003_ENG.pdf), rear differential service procedure. The same PDF separately lists transfer-case oil and W5MBB/F5MBB manual-transmission oil; those procedures must not be conflated.

## Evidence quality and applicability

This is a hosted copy of service-manual/PDI content, not a direct Mitsubishi corporate-domain publication. The precise cover revision, Russia-market applicability, model-year boundary, rear-differential assembly variant and VIN/frame scope have not been established from an authoritative publication in this pass.

Therefore the values above are source-supported candidates only. Do not mark them verified, and do not display them as definitive maintenance advice in the app until the exact applicable manual revision and vehicle configuration are confirmed.

## Service interval

This document does not establish a periodic replacement interval. A procedure appearing in a PDI/inspection manual is not by itself proof of a scheduled replacement interval. Do not infer an interval from the existence of the procedure, from another market, or from a different Outlander generation.

Required evidence for a maintenance interval:
- applicable Russian-market maintenance schedule for the exact model year / model code;
- whether the schedule classifies the item as inspection, replacement, or condition-based service;
- any severe-use conditions and their separate interval;
- source revision and page/section.

Until that is obtained, interval should remain unset (null / unverified).

## Decision

- Rear differential oil candidate: API GL-5, SAE 80W.
- Candidate quantity: 0.5 L.
- Fill level: lower edge of filler-plug hole.
- Exact applicability and maintenance interval: unresolved.
- Production vehicle data, existing records, UI and Android appId unchanged.
