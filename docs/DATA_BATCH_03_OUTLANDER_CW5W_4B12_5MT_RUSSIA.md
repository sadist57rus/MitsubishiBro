# Outlander CW5W / 4B12 — Russia market evidence (5-speed manual)

Status: **unverified / human review required**. This is a source-tracking note, not an approved technical-data pack.

## Candidate identity

- Market: Russia only; do not generalize to all CIS markets.
- Chassis: CW5W
- Engine: 4B12 DOHC MIVEC petrol, displacement listed as 2,359 mL in the 2008–2010 service-manual tables.
- Transmission: W5MBB, electronically controlled 4WD 5-speed manual transmission.
- Russian model codes shown in tables: XNSHZL6Z and XNHHZL6Z.

4. [Official Mitsubishi Motors Russia announcement, 3 March 2010](https://www.mitsubishi-motors.ru/news/news_80/) — confirms Outlander XL was sold in Russia, but is not a service specification source.

## Primary-source availability finding

The public Russian distributor portal does not currently show a CW5W-era owner manual in its visible Outlander list. The technical tables above are mirror-hosted service-manual content, not an official manufacturer-hosted copy verified for this exact edition. A factory service manual may require dealer access or a licensed service-information subscription; no access to such a primary source has been established in this pass.
