# Data batch 11 — CW5W / 4B12 Russia: maintenance interval evidence for drivetrain oils

**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**Scope:** Outlander XL / CW5W / 4B12, Russia-market candidate. Research ledger only; no production import.

## Schedule found

A third-party-hosted copy of Mitsubishi's Outlander XL *Periodic Inspection and Maintenance* PDF has a separate maintenance schedule section for petrol-powered vehicles and a later PDI publication identifier. The schedule gives the following candidate intervals:

| Maintenance item | Normal usage | Severe usage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Change manual transmission gear oil (B8) | Every 200,000 km | Every 100,000 km |
| Change transfer gear oil (B11) | Every 80,000 km | Every 40,000 km |
| Check rear differential for oil leakage / check level if leaking (B12) | Every 20,000 km or 12 months | Same listed interval |
| Change rear differential gear oil (B13) | Every 80,000 km | Every 40,000 km |

Source: [Outlander XL PDI / Periodic Inspection and Maintenance PDF copy](https://mmc-manuals.ru/manuals/outlander_xl/online/Service_Manual/spec/pdf/PDI_4753343558CW_W_11_003_ENG_100.pdf), printed pages 2-7 to 2-8 (PDF pages 30-31). The document explicitly says that when both distance and time are listed, whichever comes first applies. It also states that severe-use specifications apply only under severe operating conditions, including dusty or rough/submerged/hilly roads, cold-zone driving, prolonged idling or short cold-weather trips, frequent sudden braking, and trailer towing.

A separate 2009 PDI manual copy surfaced in search results with matching candidate values for manual gearbox and transfer oil, but exact rear-differential schedule rows and publication applicability need page-level comparison before treating the editions as identical:
[2009 Outlander XL PDI schedule PDF copy](https://faq.out-club.ru/download/outlander-xl/maintenance/Service_Manual_2008_2012/spec/pdf/4753343558CW_W_09_003_ENG.pdf)

## Applicability warning

The linked PDFs are hosted on third-party domains, not Mitsubishi corporate domains. The 2011 PDI document contains different model groups and includes special transmission types (e.g. twin-clutch SST); its schedule cannot be applied wholesale to every CW5W/4B12 trim without checking the relevant model-code table, publication revision, market, and production date. The 2009 PDF copy is also not a direct official publication and the exact Russia-market scope for these maintenance rows has not yet been independently established.

These intervals are therefore source-documented candidates, not verified production values. Do not publish them as a universal service interval for every Russian CW5W vehicle. Do not infer a calendar interval where the schedule lists only mileage.

## Keep operation types distinct

- A leak check / level check is an inspection operation, not an oil-change interval.
- A scheduled oil change is a separate operation.
- The maintenance schedule does not prove the correct oil grade or fill quantity; those remain separate evidence fields.
- The manual transmission, transfer case, and rear differential are separate service points.
- The schedule row for the manual gearbox applies only if the vehicle has the applicable manual transmission; do not apply it to the W1CJA CVT variant.

## Decision

- Candidate normal/severe intervals recorded for research: manual transmission 200,000/100,000 km; transfer case 80,000/40,000 km; rear differential oil 80,000/40,000 km.
- Rear differential leak/level inspection candidate: every 20,000 km or 12 months.
- Exact Russia-market model-year/VIN applicability: unresolved.
- Do not promote these to production data until the exact publication and model applicability are confirmed.
- No production vehicle records, UI, interfaces, or Android appId changed.
