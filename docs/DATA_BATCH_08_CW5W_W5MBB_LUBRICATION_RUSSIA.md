# Data batch 08 — CW5W / 4B12 / W5MBB Russia: transmission and transfer lubrication evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-19  
**Scope:** Russian-market Outlander XL CW5W / 4B12 / W5MBB 4WD 5-speed manual transmission only. Evidence ledger; not approved for production app data.

## Identity boundary

The 2009 workshop-manual model table lists the Russian-market CW5W / 4B12 variants with W5MBB 4WD 5MT and W1CJA 4WD CVT. This batch concerns only W5MBB. Do not apply the values below to W1CJA CVT, W6MBA diesel manuals, CW6W / 6B31, other model years without matching publication applicability, or other markets.

Sources:
- [2009 Russia model identification table](https://mmc-manuals.ru/manuals/outlander_xl/online/Service_Manual/2009/00/html/M100100030323200ENG.HTM)
- [2009 manual transmission general information and ratios](https://mmc-manuals.ru/manuals/outlander_xl/online/Service_Manual/2009/22/html/M222000010106700ENG.HTM)

## Manual gearbox oil — evidence located

The available Periodic Inspection and Maintenance PDF copy lists the following under the F5MBB/W5MBB manual transmission procedure:

- Specified transmission oil: **DIA QUEEN NEW MULTI GEAR OIL**, API GL-3, SAE 75W-80.
- Quantity: **2.5 L** for F5MBB/W5MBB.
- The procedure instructs filling until the oil reaches the lower portion of the filler-plug hole, and separately documents plug torque.

Source: [Periodic Inspection and Maintenance PDF copy](https://faq.out-club.ru/download/outlander-xl/maintenance/Service_Manual_2008_2012/spec/pdf/PDI_4753343558CW_W_12_003_ENG_100.pdf)

A second manual PDF copy also lists the same oil family and 2.5 L quantity for F5MBB/W5MBB:
[Earlier PDI PDF copy](https://mmc-manuals.ru/manuals/outlander_xl/online/Service_Manual/spec/pdf/4753343558CW8W_08_003_ENG.pdf)

These are third-party-hosted copies of service-manual content, not direct Mitsubishi corporate-domain pages. The repeated value is stronger evidence than a single mirror, but it is not a substitute for a revision-matched official publication or VIN/frame applicability check.

## Do not confuse gearbox and transfer case

The same service documentation has a separate 4WD transfer-case oil procedure. It lists a hypoid gear oil specification (API GL-5; viscosity depends on the applicable table/edition) and a separate transfer-case quantity. The transfer case is not the W5MBB gearbox: **do not combine the two quantities or display the gearbox oil specification as the transfer-case specification**.

The available source copies need a revision-specific cross-check before publishing a definitive transfer-case viscosity or quantity for an individual vehicle. No transfer-case field is approved by this batch.

## Service procedure and torque caution

A maintenance inspection PDF copy lists a tightening torque for F5MBB/W5MBB filler plugs, but torque values are safety/maintenance-critical and must be checked against the exact applicable service-manual revision before use in the app. This batch deliberately does not promote a torque value to production data.

## Decision

- W5MBB identity for the scoped Russia-market CW5W / 4B12 variant: **partially verified at manual-table level**.
- Gearbox oil candidate: DIA QUEEN NEW MULTI GEAR OIL, API GL-3, SAE 75W-80; **source-supported, not production-approved**.
- Gearbox quantity: 2.5 L; **source-supported, not production-approved**.
- Transfer case oil/quantity and service plug torque: **not approved; require revision-matched confirmation**.
- No production vehicle data, interfaces, or Android `appId` changed.
